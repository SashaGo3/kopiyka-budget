Events: a birthday, a wedding or a night out gets its own budget, like a trip, and stays out of your monthly budgets (Settings → Trips & Events). A trip or event can also go without a limit and just count what was spent.

Tap a budget or an insight to see what’s behind it, month by month. Insights adds a payments checklist that includes your recurring payments.

Search has its own screen and finds by tags, trips, events, debts and more, typos and all.

Pay a debt back in parts: pick Debt instead of a category, and the debt counts what has been paid.

Recurring payments show the company’s logo, or an icon, emoji or picture you choose. Suggested regular payments you don’t want can be dismissed for good.

Transfers: when your bank notifies both sides of a transfer between your own accounts, the two become one transfer to check. A new transfer asks for both accounts on one screen, and both amounts can be typed, so the bank’s rate or fee is kept as it was.

A payment logged from your bank’s notification keeps the bank’s words apart from your own note.

Themes named after cats: Pusik, Siri, Tadeusz & Kastuś and Una. Switching is instant, with stronger contrast.

Apple Watch: approve pending payments, add a note by dictation, delete an entry, and log from Control Center, the Smart Stack or the Action button. The watch app opens faster and now needs watchOS 26.

Plus fixes.
