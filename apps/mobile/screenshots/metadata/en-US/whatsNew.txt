Made a tyre change a recurring payment? Kopiyka used to think the next one was due… today. Again. Now six months means six months, counted from the payment you picked; changing how often it repeats moves the date too; and that payment counts as already paid, instead of the app waiting for the bank to charge you a second time.

Dismissed a suggested regular payment? It now stays gone. No more “Show 3 dismissed suggestions” lurking at the bottom like an ex.

Tags got tidier: trips and events have a page of their own (a plane for trips, a party popper for events), and archived tags moved into their own quiet room at the bottom of Settings → Tags.

A trip ends by itself the day after its last day, so the coffee back home stops being filed under the holiday.

A new launch screen: the Kopiyka К in your theme’s colours, taking a small bow on the way out. Still quick. It doesn’t wait for applause.

Plus small fixes, swept under a very small rug.
