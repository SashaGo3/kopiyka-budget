Siriusia (my cutie cat) sees the future, like your drunk, yet perspective, friend. A new insight takes your own pace, adds every currency into one, and carries it forward: when the savings goal lands, what a home would take, how much a daily coffee costs over ten years. Siriusia is a cat. It is a forecast of a very optimistic cat, not a promise, not advice. Tap her and she spins for free too.

Receipts read themselves. Hold + and the receipt camera finds the paper, takes the picture by itself, and reads the total, the shop, the address and every line. One receipt with dinner and a lamp on it becomes two entries, each in its own category. Receipts in another currency ask which account paid.
I personally don't use it often, but I love the way it works (I wish I had that 10 years ago)

Split with friends. You paid 120 for dinner for three, but only 40 was your dinner, for a wonderful math. Each friend’s share is theirs, not your spending, and their page says what they owe, with Remind for the forgetful and Let it go for the very forgetful.

Biedronka, Lidl, Сільпо, АТБ, Orlen, Uber and hundreds more shops are recognised the first time you pay there, and filed into your category of that kind, whatever you called it. The payment still waits in Pending for your "Yes, sir/madam".

One landlord, two bills: the amount now tells the rent from the service charge.

Recurring payments on a calendar: paid, late and still to come.

Also new: a spending map, three new insights, cards suggested from how you use the app, categories ordered by use or dragged into place, trips and events you can rename, and Undo instead of “Are you sure?”. You were sure, and you're sure, don't disappoint me.

Ready for iPhone Duo: folded or open, everything fits, and Save is always on screen. Why? Who knows, maybe I prepare for the 3rd generation.

Smoother all round: pickers open with the keyboard ready and a shop’s usual categories first, dragging to reorder glides, and a payment in a currency with no exchange rate, like the hryvnia, is converted as soon as a rate turns up.

Every button now answers a tap the moment your finger lands. Even the ones that used to pretend they hadn’t noticed.
