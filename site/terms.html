What the app is for

Kopiyka is a personal record-keeping tool. It stores numbers you type in and does arithmetic on them.

It is not financial, tax, accounting, investment, or legal advice, and nothing it shows you — a budget, an insight card, a projection, a total — is a recommendation. Decisions about your money are yours. If a figure matters, check it against your bank's own statement.

Your data is your responsibility

Everything Kopiyka stores lives on your device. It sends nothing to the developer, who therefore holds no copy of your data and cannot restore it for you. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and where.

Kopiyka can write backups into your own iCloud Drive, and this is strongly recommended — but iCloud is Apple's service, not the developer's. Whether a backup uploads, stays available, or can be read back depends on your iCloud account, your storage quota, your network, and Apple's service. Keeping a copy of your own data is ultimately your responsibility. Settings → Data management can export everything at any time as JSON, CSV, or a ZIP that includes your receipt photos; do that before anything irreversible, such as replacing your data with a file or resetting the app.

Exchange rates

When Kopiyka needs to convert between currencies it uses European Central Bank reference rates, fetched from a free public service. These are indicative reference rates. They are not the rate your bank or card issuer will actually apply to a transaction, they may be a day old or older, and no guarantee is made about their accuracy or availability. Where the app can use a figure your bank itself printed, it prefers that figure over a converted one.

The Shortcuts automation is beta

Kopiyka can be set up, through Apple's Shortcuts app, to read payment notifications your bank sends you and turn them into entries. This feature is beta.

It works by parsing the text of a notification — banks word their notifications however they like, and change that wording without warning. So it may misread an amount, pick the wrong shop or account, miss a payment entirely, or occasionally record something that was not a payment. Entries it creates are deliberately held in the Pending queue for exactly this reason. Review them before you rely on them.

Kopiyka has no connection to your bank and cannot make, stop, or alter any payment.
