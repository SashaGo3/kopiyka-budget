## Exchange rates

When Kopiyka needs to convert between currencies it uses European Central Bank reference rates,
fetched from a free public service. These are **indicative reference rates**. They are not the
rate your bank or card issuer will actually apply to a transaction, they may be a day old or
older, and no guarantee is made about their accuracy or availability. Where the app can use a
figure your bank itself printed, it prefers that figure over a converted one.

## The Shortcuts automation is beta

Kopiyka can be set up, through Apple's Shortcuts app, to read payment notifications your bank
sends you and turn them into entries. This feature is **beta**.

It works by parsing the text of a notification — banks word their notifications however they like,
and change that wording without warning. So it may misread an amount, pick the wrong shop or
account, miss a payment entirely, or occasionally record something that was not a payment. Entries
it creates are deliberately held in the Pending queue for exactly this reason. **Review them
before you rely on them.**

Kopiyka has no connection to your bank and cannot make, stop, or alter any payment.
